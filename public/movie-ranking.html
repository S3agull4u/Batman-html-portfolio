My three favourite movies of all time

personal favourite of Batman

1. Ghost(1990)

Ghost is a 1990 American romantic fantasy thriller film directed by Jerry Zucker, produced by Bruce Joel Rubin and starring Patrick Swayze, Demi Moore, Whoopi Goldberg, Tony Goldwyn, Rick Aviles, and Vincent Schiavelli.

2. Terminator (1984)

The Terminator is a 1984 American science fiction action film directed by James Cameron and starring Arnold Schwarzenegger, Linda Hamilton, and Michael Biehn. It tells the story of a cyborg assassin sent back in time to kill Sarah Connor, whose unborn son will lead humanity in a war against machines.

3. Rambo (1982)

Rambo is a 1982 American war action film directed by George P. Cosmatos and starring Sylvester Stallone. It follows the story of John Rambo, a former U.S. Army Special Forces operative who is hunted by the military after going on a rampage in Vietnam.
